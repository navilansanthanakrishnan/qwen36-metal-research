Two things measured here, not inherited

  • The Q4_K half-precision scale division is worse than reported. Over 14,919,680 super-blocks of this model, median d = 6.187e-05, so d/16 = 3.87e-06 is subnormal in half for 99.9998% of blocks — median relative error 3.66e-03 (~8 mantissa bits), p99 9.6e-03, and some blocks flush to zero outright.
  • The verify-cost staircase. K-quant weight streams per verify step, verified against the pinned source: depth 1 → 2 streams, depth 2 → 3, depths 3 and 4 → 1, depths 5–7 → 2, depth 8+ → 1. Choosing the depth is free and worth up to 2–3×.
